Common Cyber Threats

Recognize the risks before they become a problem.

🎣

Phishing

Fake emails, messages, or websites designed to trick people into sharing passwords or personal information.

🦠

Malware

Harmful software that can damage devices, steal information, or disrupt normal computer operations.

🔑

Password Attacks

Attempts to gain access to accounts using stolen, weak, or reused passwords.

🎭

Social Engineering

Manipulating people into revealing confidential information or performing unsafe actions.

Stay Safe Online

Simple habits that make a big difference.

🔑

Use Strong Passwords

Create unique passwords for every account. Use a password manager to keep them secure.

📲

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Add an extra layer of protection to your accounts using 2FA or a passkey.

🔍

Verify Links Before Clicking

Check the sender and website address carefully. Avoid unexpected links and attachments.

📡

Be Careful With Public Wi-Fi

Avoid sensitive transactions on unfamiliar networks. Use trusted connections whenever possible.

🔄

Keep Software Updated

Install updates for your operating system, browser, and applications to fix security vulnerabilities.

🚨

Report Suspicious Activity

If you notice a scam or compromised account, report it to the relevant platform or trusted authority.
